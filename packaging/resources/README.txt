Jarvis 1.0.0

Installs Jarvis in /Applications. The full package includes Ollama and the qwen2.5:3b model; first AI setup copies the model into the current user's local Ollama cache. Internet access is not required for local inference. Supported Macs run macOS 14 or later and have Apple Silicon or Intel processors. About 8 GB RAM and 6 GB free disk space are recommended. Model speed depends on hardware.
